### 2. Technical Recommendations & High-Impact Improvements

#### A. Multi-Viewport / Hanging Layouts Early (Before `app.rs` Grows Too Large)
Right now, [ViewerApp](file:///d:/Documents/AntiGravity/rdicom-project/rdicom/src/app.rs#L58-L102) holds a single `selected` series, a single `slice`, a single `window`, a single `cache`, and a single `Uploaded` GPU texture in [gpu_view.rs](file:///d:/Documents/AntiGravity/rdicom-project/rdicom/src/gpu_view.rs#L127).
- **Why this matters now:** In daily radiology reading, side-by-side comparison (1×2, 2×2, T1 vs. T2, pre- vs. post-contrast, prior vs. current, scout with cross-reference line) is essential.
- **Recommendation:** Before adding lots of tools to `ViewerApp`, extract a `ViewportState` struct (holding series, slice, W/L, zoom/pan, active ROIs) and change [gpu_view.rs](file:///d:/Documents/AntiGravity/rdicom-project/rdicom/src/gpu_view.rs#L127) from `current: Option<Uploaded>` to a small LRU map `HashMap<SliceKey, Uploaded>`.   3. The exact 2D/3D coordinate foundations that `IsiXROI` and later **MPR** depend on.

#### C. Multi-Frame DICOM Performance Bottleneck in `frame.rs`
Look at [frame.rs](file:///d:/Documents/AntiGravity/rdicom-project/rdicom/src/frame.rs#L115-L122) and [dicom_index.rs](file:///d:/Documents/AntiGravity/rdicom-project/rdicom/src/dicom_index.rs#L308-L316):
- For a multi-frame file (e.g., ultrasound cine loop, cardiac MRI, angiography, breast tomosynthesis, or enhanced CT/MR with 200 frames in one file), `load_frame(path, frame)` re-opens and re-parses the entire multi-megabyte file from disk for *every single frame*.
- **Recommendation:** Cache the opened `DefaultDicomObject` (or decode multi-frame pixel data in batches) when `NumberOfFrames > 1`. That will make multi-frame cine loops open in milliseconds instead of re-reading a 500 MB file 200 times.

#### D. Slice Sorting Subtlety in `dicom_index.rs`
In [dicom_index.rs](file:///d:/Documents/AntiGravity/rdicom-project/rdicom/src/dicom_index.rs#L359-L374), `sort_slices` currently sorts by **Instance Number first**, and only uses spatial position (`dot(position, normal)`) as a tie-breaker:
```rust
a.instance.cmp(&b.instance)
    .then_with(|| key(a).partial_cmp(&key(b))...)
```
- In real-world studies (and in Horos/IsiX), `InstanceNumber` is occasionally out of spatial order or duplicated across phases/recons, whereas sorting by **spatial projection along the slice normal** (or user-selectable spatial vs. instance sorting) is what prevents anatomical "jumping" when scrolling a stack and is required for building a contiguous 3D volume for MPR. We should check IsiX's exact slice-sorting rule in `IsiXDatabase`/`IsiXImaging` and match it.

#### E. GPU Texture Upload & Volume Caching on Your RTX 5080
- Your RTX 5080 has 16 GB of ultra-fast GDDR7 VRAM. A 1,000-slice 512×512 CT series in 16-bit integer is only **500 MB** (or 1 GB in `f32`).
- Currently, slices are kept in system RAM and uploaded 2D slice-by-slice to the GPU. When we reach **MPR / Thick-Slab MIP**, we can upload the entire series once into a single `wgpu` 3D texture (`R16Sint` or `R32Float`), and your RTX 5080 will reslice oblique planes and render thick-slab MIPs at 240+ FPS with almost zero CPU usage.

### 2. Local Database: Why OsiriX / IsiX Never Crash (and Best Way for SekhmetX)
- **OsiriX and IsiX actually use SQLite under the hood** (via Apple Core Data's `NSSQLiteStoreType` in `Database.sql`).
- **Why SQLite crashes in some apps (and how to prevent it 100%):**
  SQLite itself is rock-solid when used properly; crashes/corruption happen when apps open multiple concurrent write connections without locking, hold long transactions, or stuff giant pixel blobs into the DB.
- **Best design for SekhmetX:**
  1. **Store metadata + file paths only** in the database (Patients, Studies, Series, Slice paths, Albums, Key Images) — keep actual DICOM files on disk just like Horos/IsiX.
  2. **Crash-safe engine:**
     - **Option A (`rusqlite` with `bundled` SQLite):** Enable `PRAGMA journal_mode = WAL; PRAGMA synchronous = NORMAL; PRAGMA busy_timeout = 5000;` and serialize writes through a single dedicated DB worker/connection. This is ACID-atomic (power-loss safe) and handles 100,000+ studies effortlessly.
     - **Option B (`redb`, pure-Rust embedded B-tree):** Zero C code, copy-on-write crash-safe transactions by design, and single-file simplicity.
  - Either way, **SQLite in WAL mode with a single writer** matches IsiX/Horos's exact model, supports instant SQL search/sorting/albums, and won't corrupt on crashes.
